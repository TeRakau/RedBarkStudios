FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a website take?

Most small business sites and landing pages take one to three weeks depending on size and how quickly content comes together.

What is clipping?

Clipping means cutting the best moments out of long-form content — podcasts, streams, interviews — into short vertical videos. Platforms like Content Rewards pay clippers based on the views those clips earn for the creator or brand.

Can you clip for my own channel?

Yes. Send your long-form videos and I'll deliver captioned, ready-to-post clips on a schedule that suits you.

Do you offer ongoing work?

Yes — there are three monthly plans: Website Care (updates, backups, uptime checks, small edits), Creator Clipping (a set number of clips each month) and Content Support (a set number of edits and exports).

How do I pay?

You'll get a clear quote up front. Larger web projects are usually split into a deposit and a final payment on launch.
