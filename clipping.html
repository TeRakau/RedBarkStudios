FAQ

Clipping questions.

What is clipping?

Clipping means cutting the best moments out of long-form content, such as podcasts, streams and interviews, into short vertical videos. Platforms like Content Rewards pay clippers based on the views those clips earn.

What do I need to send?

Links to your long-form videos (YouTube, Twitch VODs, podcasts or files), plus any brand guidelines or campaign brief.

Can you clip for my own channel?

Yes. I deliver captioned, ready-to-post clips on a schedule that suits you, and can post them for you by agreement.

Where can I see examples?

My clips run on Clippyton, the RedBarkStudios clipping channel, on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
