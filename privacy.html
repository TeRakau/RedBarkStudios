Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This page explains what information Red Bark Studios collects through redbarkstudios.com, why, and what happens to it. The short version: I only collect what you choose to send me, and I only use it to reply to you and work on your project.

What I collect

When you use the contact form, I receive:

  • Your name and email address
  • The service you picked and the project details you write

If you email me directly, I receive whatever you include in that email.

How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a quote
  • To plan, deliver and support any project we agree on
  • To keep basic records for invoices and taxes

I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Services that help run this site

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to my inbox.
  • Gmail is where those messages and emails are stored.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on this site, which means your browser connects to Google when the page loads.
  • Web hosting and Cloudflare serve the site and may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, for security and performance.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set its own cookies or use advertising trackers. If I add privacy-friendly analytics later, I'll update this page.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into a project are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept as long as needed for the project and for tax and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time. Just email redbarkstudios@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
